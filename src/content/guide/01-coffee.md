The Rivelia upstairs is honestly hard to beat, but if you want to get out of the house:

- **[Bellwood Coffee](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bellwood+Coffee+Midtown+Atlanta)** — good for a slow morning.
- **[Agora Midtown](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Agora+Midtown+Atlanta)** — on Peachtree Pl. Bring a laptop.
- **[Spiller Park Coffee](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Spiller+Park+Coffee+Ponce+City+Market)** — inside Ponce City Market, right on the BeltLine. Make an outing of it.
- **[Dancing Goats Coffee Bar](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dancing+Goats+Coffee+Bar+Ponce+City+Market)** — also at Ponce, for when Spiller's line is out the door.
