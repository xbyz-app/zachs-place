Midtown is very walkable for dinner. A few I'd send you to:

- **[Empire State South](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Empire+State+South+Atlanta)** — modern Southern on Peachtree. Breakfast, lunch or dinner.
- **[South City Kitchen Midtown](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=South+City+Kitchen+Midtown+Atlanta)** — the fried chicken and shrimp & grits you came to Atlanta for.
- **[Mary Mac's Tea Room](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mary+Macs+Tea+Room+Atlanta)** — an Atlanta institution since 1945. Get the pot likker.
- **[Lure](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lure+Midtown+Atlanta)** — seafood, on Crescent Ave.
- **[Lazy Betty](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lazy+Betty+Atlanta)** — a tasting menu, for when it's a special night. Book ahead.
- **[Lewis Barbecue](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lewis+Barbecue+Atlanta+BeltLine)** — Texas barbecue on the BeltLine.
- **[Ponce City Market](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ponce+City+Market)** — a food hall with something for everyone, when nobody can decide.
- **[The Varsity](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Varsity+North+Avenue+Atlanta)** — chili dogs and frosted oranges on North Ave. "What'll ya have?" is the whole menu experience.
