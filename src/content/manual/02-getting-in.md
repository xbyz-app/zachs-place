**Callbox** — one guest callbox, on the **Spring St** side. Either scroll to **"Strohmeyer, Z"**, or type the **last 4 of Zach's phone number** as a search shortcut (much faster). Press Call, Zach picks up and presses **9** to unlock.

> **The part that catches everyone: getting buzzed in only gets you to the lobby.** Floor access on the elevator is controlled, so you'll get inside and stop there. Either Zach comes down and taps you up, or you call the **concierge desk at (678) 992-4175** and ask them to release the floor.

**Once you have the keychain**, it has the elevator and door code and you're on your own.

**Food delivery** — the concierge will accept it, but they won't bring it upstairs. Point the driver at the Spring St callbox and plan to walk down to the lobby.
