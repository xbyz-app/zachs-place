- Overhead light and fan are on **wall switches**.
- **Nightstand lamps** are dimmable — twist the base.
- Everything else is automated; the controls at the top of this page let you tweak the vibe in your room.
- If something looks off and you just want lights ON: ask the Google Home Hub, _"Hey Google, set Chill in the guest room."_
