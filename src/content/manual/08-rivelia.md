The **DeLonghi Rivelia** on the counter does basically anything. Two **bean hoppers** swap on top — try both.

**Operation:** Turn the machine on, let it cycle through warm-up — **don't put a mug under until after.** Then tap a profile, tap the drink, adjust if you want, press Start.

**Mugs:** cabinet above the machine.

**Milk:** carafe in the fridge. After every milk drink, **rotate the dial on the carafe lid to "Clean"** and press Start to run the auto-clean cycle — otherwise the next drink tastes off.

**Tea & hot water:** the machine makes plain hot water. Use the hot water spout (in the same slot the milk carafe goes into).

> **Yellow error?** Give the drip tray a small nudge to the right.
