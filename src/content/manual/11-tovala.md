The black countertop oven is a **Tovala Smart Oven** — steam + bake + broil + toast.

**Quick mode pick:**

- **Reheat** — leftovers
- **Steam** — fresh, moisture-sensitive food (fill the side reservoir first; it'll prompt "Add Water" if not)
- **Toast** — bread/bagels, levels 1–5
- **Bake** — from-scratch (set temp + time)
- **Scan** — for Tovala Meals; scan the barcode on the box

Rack stays in the **lowest** position with curved edges down. Drain tray is at the front — slides out.
