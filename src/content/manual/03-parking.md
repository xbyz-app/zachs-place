**There is no free guest parking, and no street-permit workaround** — every spot in the garage is assigned and paid through the **Parkade** app.

If you're driving, tell Zach ahead of time and he'll book you one: Parkade → Find parking → set the times → pick a spot → under **Who's parking?** choose **Someone else**, leave the vehicle field on "Guest will set their own car," and send you the link. You add your own plate from the link and get an **Open gate** button.

Anything that goes wrong at the garage — gate won't open, someone's in the spot — goes to `hello@parkade.com`, not the leasing office.
