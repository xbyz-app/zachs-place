The **DeLonghi Rivelia** coffee machine on the counter will make you very nearly anything. There are two **bean hoppers** that swap out on top — try both, that's the entire point of them.

**How to run it:** turn the machine on and let it finish its warm-up cycle. **Don't put a mug underneath until that's done.** Tap your drink, adjust whatever you like, and press Start.

**Mugs** are in the cabinet directly above the machine.

**Milk drinks:** the milk lives in its carafe in the fridge. Pull the hot water spout out of the slot on the front of the machine and push the carafe's spout into that same slot until it clicks. Then pick your drink. After every milk drink, **turn the dial on the carafe lid to "Clean"** and press Start to run the auto-clean. Skip it and the next drink tastes haunted. Then put the carafe back in the fridge and the hot water spout back in its slot.

**Tea & hot water:** check that the hot water spout is in the slot (not the milk carafe), then pick Hot Water.
