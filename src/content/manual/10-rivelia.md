The **DeLonghi Rivelia** on the counter will make you very nearly anything. There are two **bean hoppers** that swap out on top — try both, that's the entire point of them.

**How to run it:** turn the machine on and let it finish its warm-up cycle. **Don't put a mug underneath until that's done.** Then tap a profile, tap your drink, adjust whatever you like, and press Start.

**Mugs** are in the cabinet directly above the machine.

**Milk** lives in the carafe in the fridge. After every milk drink, **rotate the dial on the carafe lid to "Clean"** and press Start to run the auto-clean cycle. Skip it and the next drink tastes haunted.

**Tea & hot water** — it makes plain hot water too. Use the hot water spout, which goes into the same slot as the milk carafe.
