- Overhead light and fan are on **wall switches**.
- **Nightstand lamps** are dimmable — twist the base.
- Your room's accent lights (TV bars, the IG logo, and the two lights in the guest bath) are on the controls at the top of this page. While you're staying here they're yours — the house won't override them.
- Everything else in the apartment is automated and drifts warmer and dimmer as the night goes on. That's on purpose.

> **Leave the wall switches ON.** The smart bulbs need power to hear commands. Flip a switch off and that bulb drops off the network and stops responding to everything until it's flipped back on.

If a room looks wrong and you just want light: ask a Google Home Hub, _"Hey Google, set Chill,"_ or text Zach.
