- The overhead light and the fan are on **wall switches**, like it's 1997.
- The **nightstand lamps** dim when you twist the base, and the base has **USB ports**, so your phone can charge right next to you.
- The **little button remote** on the bathroom counter has a button for the bathroom lights: one press turns both the Live Nudes sign and the lava lamp on or off.
- Your room's accent lights (the TV bars, the IG logo, and the two lights in the guest bath) are also on the controls at the top of this page. Set them however you like and they stay that way until morning.
- Want the living room cozy? Tap **Welcome** on the tablet in the living room and it sets warm, low lights out there and in the entry.
- Everything else in the apartment runs itself off whatever it knows: the weather, the time of day, how bright it already is, who's home, what's on my calendar, and more. This is Zach's home, after all.
